--- SELECTION RATIONALE (UNSUPERVISED EVIDENCE ONLY) ---
satisfaction plays no part in this decision.
Elbow (largest marginal inertia drop) : k = 3
Best k by Silhouette Score            : k = 2 (0.1232)
Best k by Davies-Bouldin Index        : k = 7 (2.1377)
Best k by Calinski-Harabasz Index     : k = 2 (1,426.4)
Selected k                             : k = 3
The three indices do NOT necessarily agree. k=3 is selected as the elbow with a
near-optimal silhouette, a highly ranked CH, balanced cluster sizes and the simplest
model among the near-optimal candidates. Its Davies-Bouldin is NOT the sweep optimum;
that disagreement is accepted and documented rather than hidden.

Absolute internal indices are modest, and the PCA projection shows substantial overlap.

isolated natural clusters. The PCA overlap is supportive evidence only, because the
first two components retain a limited share of total variance.
