--- METHODOLOGY NOTES ---
- Unsupervised: no target used during clustering; satisfaction is post-hoc only.
- No train/test split: no target is predicted; structure is discovered.
- Standard supervised k-fold CV is not directly applicable;
  internal indices + linkage comparison + dendrogram + reproducible sampling are used.
- X.csv is already standardised; standardization is not repeated.
- Euclidean distance (required by Ward, appropriate for standardized features).
- PCA and t-SNE are visualization only; neither determines k/linkage nor assesses quality.
- random_state = 42 governs all sampling and stochastic steps.

--- LIMITATIONS ---
- Agglomerative metrics are sample-based (5,000 rows), not full-data.
- The dendrogram is drawn from a 1,000-row sub-sample (truncated view).
- Internal indices for bounded ordinal survey data are expected to be modest.
- Chi-square significance is not a measure of clustering quality.
